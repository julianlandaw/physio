Model scope

The model includes a well-mixed breathing circuit, alveoli, and vessel-rich, muscle, and fat tissue groups. It uses partial-pressure fractions, blood:gas solubility, cardiac output, and tissue perfusion. It does not model patient effort, V/Q mismatch, shunt, metabolism, compound A, vaporizer mechanics, scavenging, or measured gas analysis. It is for teaching—not clinical vaporizer setting or patient management.
